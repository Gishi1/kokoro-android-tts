# Mixed realistic Android TTS corpus

Authored test fixture. Every line is a plausible thing a phone would read aloud.
The point is coverage of the token-cost classes that break a character-based
budget, measured in docs/research/calibration.json:

  prose letters      ~1.0  tokens/char
  digits / money     ~3.2-3.5
  acronym runs       ~2.1-2.3
  URLs and paths     ~2.1-2.4
  CJK                ~13-14

One utterance per line. Blank lines and lines starting with `#` are ignored by
the harness.
